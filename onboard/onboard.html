Welcome to AitherConnect

Three steps to wire your browser into the AitherOS platform.

1 · Sign in
2 · Pick a mode
3 · Provision

Sign in to portal.aitherium.com

Your portal account is the identity used to scope agents, workspaces, and federated fleet membership. Don't have one yet? Create one.

Choose a deployment mode

You can change this later from the extension Settings page.

☁️ Cloud only easiest
All inference and storage runs on portal.aitherium.com. Nothing to install locally. Best for trying AitherOS without a workstation setup.
🔌 Hybrid (local + portal) recommended
AitherShell and AitherNode run on your machine; portal coordinates identity, fleet, and shared knowledge. Best for power users.
🖥️ Local only
Self-host the entire AitherOS stack on your machine. No portal traffic. Use this for air-gapped or offline development.
