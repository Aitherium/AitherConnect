☁️ Cloud only easiest
All inference and storage runs on portal.aitherium.com. Nothing to install locally. Best for trying AitherOS without a workstation setup.
🔌 Hybrid (local + portal) recommended
AitherShell and awnode run on your machine; portal coordinates identity, fleet, and shared knowledge. Best for power users.
🖥️ Local only
Self-host the entire AitherOS stack on your machine. No portal traffic. Use this for air-gapped or offline development.
