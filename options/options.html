On-Page Aither

What Awconnect draws into the websites you visit. The side panel is unaffected by these — it is the extension's own UI.

Aither Command Bar
Injects the Aither bar into every page (never onto aitherium.com itself — the Living OS renders its own dock there). On x.com and linkedin.com this bar is also what drives the social automation: turning it off stops posting and engagement. Use the bar's own hide control to dismiss it on this browser without disabling automation.
AitherOS Overlay (experimental)
Iframes the Living OS chrome over the current page. Requires the site half (?mode=overlay + os-regions) to be live on the apex; until then the overlay paints an opaque desktop over the page. Leave off unless you are testing it.
On-device model (Bonsai)
Lets this extension download a Bonsai model (236 MB – 3.6 GB) and run it on your GPU, in the background, for on-device composing and replies. Nothing is sent to a server for it. Off until you turn it on — an unattended multi-gigabyte download is not something an extension may decide for you. Turning it off stops future loads; already-downloaded weights stay cached until you clear site data.
